Side Arm Raise

Exercise Goal

Objective

Improve shoulder abduction through controlled movement while maintaining correct posture throughout the exercise.

Primary Muscle

Middle Deltoid

Movement

Shoulder Abduction

Target

Reach the rehabilitation target ROM safely.

Instructions

  1. Stand upright facing the camera.
  2. Keep your feet shoulder-width apart.
  3. Raise your surgery arm sideways.
  4. Reach the highlighted target angle.
  5. Hold the position until instructed.
  6. Lower the arm slowly.
  7. Repeat until all repetitions are completed.
Progress depends on completing rehabilitation goals rather than calendar weeks.

Metrics Tracked

Metric Description
Range of Motion Highest shoulder angle achieved.
Target Angle Required ROM for current stage.
Hold Time Successful hold duration.
Repetitions Total successful repetitions.
Session Progress Overall completion percentage.
Recovery Trend Improvement over previous sessions.

Success Tips

  • Remain standing throughout the exercise.
  • Keep your full upper body visible.
  • Move slowly and avoid momentum.
  • Do not shrug your shoulders.
  • Maintain an upright posture.
  • Breathe normally.
  • Stop immediately if sharp pain occurs.

Ready?

Ensure your camera is positioned correctly and there is enough room to perform the movement safely.
