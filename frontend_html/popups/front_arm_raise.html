Side Arm Raise

Exercise Goal

Objective

Restore shoulder mobility through controlled arm movements while gradually rebuilding strength, endurance, and range of motion by bringing the orchestra back to life.

Primary Muscle

Middle Deltoid (Primary) Supraspinatus (Assists shoulder abduction)

Movement

Shoulder Abduction

Target

Reach the rehabilitation target Range of Motion (ROM) while unlocking all musical instruments through smooth, controlled movements.

Instructions

  1. Stand upright facing the camera.
  2. Keep your entire upper body visible to the camera.
  3. Slowly raise your surgery arm until you reach the target shoulder angle.
  4. Hold your arm steady to unlock musical instruments.
  5. Complete the required range of motion, hold time, and repetitions to finish the current rehabilitation stage.
  6. Once all rehabilitation targets are achieved, you unlock the next stage of your recovery journey.
Progress depends on completing rehabilitation goals rather than calendar weeks.

Metrics Tracked

Metric Description
Range of Motion Highest shoulder angle achieved.
Target Angle Required ROM for current stage.
Hold Time Successful hold duration.
Repetitions Total successful repetitions.

Success Tips

  • Remain standing throughout the exercise.
  • Keep your full upper body visible.
  • Move slowly and avoid momentum.
  • Do not shrug your shoulders.
  • Maintain an upright posture.
  • Breathe normally.
  • Stop immediately if sharp pain occurs.

Ready?

Ensure your camera is positioned correctly and there is enough room to perform the movement safely.
